AFFINE LAB — FIVE-MINUTE DEMO

Goal in simple words
We show why an Affine substitution cipher cannot protect a conversation.
The client sends an encoded username and concern to a local server. The server
decodes them, gets an English reply, encrypts that reply and sends it back.
A separate analyzer sees only the reply ciphertext, tries 312 valid keys,
scores their English letter frequencies and recovers the most plausible text.
After recovery, independent checks compare it with the client and known inputs.

6. EXPLAIN THE MATH
A=0, B=1, ... Z=25.
Encryption: C = (a*P+b) mod 26.
Decryption: P = inverse(a)*(C-b) mod 26.
a multiplies the letter number; b shifts it. gcd(a,26) must be 1.
There are 12 valid multipliers and 26 shifts: 12*26 = 312 keys.
For a=7,b=6, inverse(a)=15 because 7*15=105=4*26+1.
E=4 becomes I=8; T=19 becomes J=9.
15a=1 (mod26), so a=7; b=8-4*7 mod26=6.
These are correspondences of the winning candidate, not an assumption that
the two most frequent letters are always E and T.
Chi-square = sum((observed-expected)^2/expected), over the 26 letters.
Expected = total letters * English frequency / 100. Smaller scores fit better.
The analyzer counts the ciphertext once and permutes 26 bins for each key,
instead of decrypting the full message 312 times. It then decrypts the winner.

7. TERMS
Plaintext: readable original text. Ciphertext: substituted letters.
Affine key: the pair (a,b). Modular inverse: the multiplier that undoes a.
Nibble armor: each UTF-8 byte becomes two A–P letters before substitution;
this reversible representation is encoding, not extra cryptographic security.
The mood schedule is public and weak too, but the analyzer does not use mood
to recover a key. It is consulted only in the separate verification stage.

9. LIMITS TO SAY HONESTLY
Short/non-English text can rank badly. A top score is not a probability.
These five concerns are synthetic and replies are not clinical advice.
The PCAP reader handles this lab's classic IPv4 loopback captures, not all
network protocols. There is no claim to decrypt TLS or attack outside systems.
The 24 portfolio figures are generated from actual data, reports and source.
